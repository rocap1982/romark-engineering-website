## What is turning?

Metal turning is a basic machining process that is used to cut away excess material to form a component’s desired dimensions. We use a turning machine or lathe to remove metal pieces from a rotating part’s outer diameter at high speeds. This precise process allows us to produce a range of complex elements and parts suitable for various industries.

## CNC Machining for Turning

Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machining is a manufacturing process whereby pre-programmed software dictates the movement of the lathe and tools. Our advanced machinery is used to create precise metal parts and components by controlling a range of complex machinery, from grinders and lathes to mills and CNC routers. With CNC machining, three-dimensional cutting tasks can be accomplished in a single set of prompts.

We create bespoke metalwork fabrication using our advanced software and methods – from one-off projects to full production runs of components. We can also design and produce CNC machined tooling tailored to your specific requirements and offer a 2D and 3D CAD service for various uses, from reverse engineering projects to producing drawings for manufacture. Manufacturing for both one-off and production volumes for the automotive, power and architectural industries, our highly skilled workforce produces prototype parts and pressed parts for production runs.

## What are the benefits of turning?

### [Flexibility](#)

While most commonly used for metalwork, turning machinery can accommodate a range of materials. This makes it a very flexible process suitable for metal, wood, plastic and much more.

### [High Tolerances](#)

Turning can create parts with incredibly high tolerances. Due to this, turning is often undertaken to add precision rotational features to a component where a basic shape has already been formed.

### [Productivity](#)

Turning is ideal for producing individual pieces in small or large batches. As it can produce complex shapes in a range of quantities, we can control the operation and increase productivity.

### [Accuracy & Zero Defects](#)

We programme the desired cuts into the software programme, which are then dictated to the corresponding turning machinery and tools. This means that all dimensional tasks are carried out to precise specifications with zero defects.

### [Efficiency](#)

Using One CNC CAD-CAM, a market leader programming system, we deliver high quality, tight tolerances and improved efficiency. You can trust that your products are in safe hands with us.

### [Cost-Effective](#)

Our turning machinery is highly cost-effective. As the machinery is so accurate, waste is kept to a minimum and no raw materials will be thrown away. This means that you will save money every time you come to us.
