## What are MIG and TIG aluminium welding in London?

### TIG Welding

Tungsten inert gas (TIG) welding is used to create joins between several small and thin materials. TIG aluminium welding in London uses a non-consumable tungsten electrode to heat the metal and can be used with or without a filler. Though a slower process than MIG welding, TIG aluminium welding in London boasts precision and accuracy.

### MIG Welding

Metal inert gas (MIG) aluminium welding in London is used to weld together larger, thicker materials. MIG aluminium welding in London uses a consumable wire that acts as both the electrode and the filler material. In this way, it can be faster than TIG welding, though it is not quite as precise or clean as TIG welding results.
