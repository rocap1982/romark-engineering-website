## What is laser cutting in Essex?

Laser cutting in Essex generates a concentrated beam of light through a cutting lens to cut metal. By focusing the light into a small and hot focal point, laser cutting in Essex can cut metals including steel, stainless, aluminium, copper and brass. If you’re looking for high-specification, precision laser cutting in Essex, then you’ve found the answer with us.

* +/- 0.1mm tolerances in many thicknesses.
* Reduced localised heat distortion.
* Polished edge finish on stainless steel.
