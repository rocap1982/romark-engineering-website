## What is laser cutting in Basildon?

Laser cutting in Basildon uses a beam of light concentrated through a cutting lens to cut materials, usually metals. By focusing the light into a small and hot focal point, laser cutting in Basildon can cut metals including steel, stainless, aluminium, copper and brass. The precision laser cutting in Basildon that we provide lets us deliver high-specification and highly optimised metal parts and components.
