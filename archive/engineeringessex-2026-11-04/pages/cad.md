## What is CAD?

Computer-aided design or CAD is one of the first steps in the process of visualising your part or component. Using computer software, we can create a 3D image to map out the shape and dimensions of the part. The process of computer-aided design combined with computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) enables us to create precise and detailed metal parts that are suitable for a range of industries.

## Precision Machining with CAD Software

## What are the benefits of CAD Software?

### [Accuracy & Speed](#)

Our CAD software is advanced and boasts precision and accuracy. A major benefit of using CAD is that we can check the tool path before the material is cut, saving you the cost of making an incorrect cut into an expensive metal.

### [Planning](#)

CAD allows for design development, alteration and optimisation for a range of parts and components. Computer-aided design lets us create more precise designs and manipulate them virtually for accurate planning.

### [Productivity](#)

We can produce the CAD drawing from scratch or do full blank developments depending on your needs. Once the design has been programmed, the CAD software retains the pattern. This means that it can be reused and can increase productivity.

### [Efficiency](#)

Using One CNC CAD-CAM, a market leader programming system, we deliver high quality, tight tolerances and improved efficiency. You can trust that your products are in safe hands with us.

### [Cost-Effective](#)

Our CAD software and machinery are highly cost-effective. With waste being kept to a minimum and no raw materials being thrown away, you will save money every time you come to us.

## CAD & CNC Machining | Precision & Speed

Used alongside the CAD process, Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machining is a manufacturing process whereby pre-programmed software dictates the movement of tools and machinery. Our advanced machinery is used to create precise metal parts and components by controlling a range of complex machinery, from grinders and lathes to mills and CNC routers. With CNC machining, three-dimensional cutting tasks can be accomplished in a single set of prompts.
