## Precision Engineering in Essex with CAD Software& CNC

### What is CAD Software?

Computer-aided design or CAD is one of the first steps in the process of visualising your part or component. Using computer software, we can create a 3D image to map out the shape and dimensions of the part. The process of computer-aided design combined with computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) enables us to create precision engineering in Essex that is suitable for many industries. Using One CNC CAD-CAM, a market leader programming system, we deliver high standards, tight tolerances and precision engineering in Essex.

### What is CNC Machining?

Used in conjunction with CAD, Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machining is a process whereby pre-programmed software dictates the movement of tools and equipment. Our high-performance machinery delivers precision engineering in Essex by controlling a range of advanced tools such as grinders, lathes, mills and more. With CNC machining, three-dimensional cutting tasks can be accomplished in a single set of prompts.
