Computer-aided design (CAD) is usually the first step in the process of creating your part or component. Using computer software, we design a 3D image to ascertain the dimensions of the part. ### What is CNC Machining?

Used in conjunction with CAD, Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machining uses pre-programmed software to dictate the movement of tools and equipment. Our advanced machinery delivers precision engineering in Kent by controlling a range of tools such as grinders, lathes, mills and more. With CNC machining, three-dimensional cutting tasks can be accomplished in a single set of prompts.
