## Precision Machining with CAD Software& CNC

### CAD Software

We create bespoke sheetmetal work using our advanced CAD software – from one-off projects to full production runs of CNC machined components. We can also design and produce CNC machined tooling tailored to your specific requirements and offer a 2D and 3D CAD service for various uses, from reverse engineering projects to producing drawings for manufacture. Using One CNC CAD-CAM, a market leader programming system, we deliver high quality, tight tolerances and improved efficiency.

### CNC Machining

Used alongside the CAD process, Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machining is a manufacturing process whereby pre-programmed software dictates the movement of tools and machinery. Our advanced machinery is used to create precise sheetmetal work by controlling a range of complex machinery, from grinders and lathes to mills and CNC routers. With CNC machining, three-dimensional cutting tasks can be accomplished in a single set of prompts.
