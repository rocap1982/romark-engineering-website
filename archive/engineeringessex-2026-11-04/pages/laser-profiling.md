## What is laser profiling?

Laser profiling uses a beam of light concentrated through a cutting lens to cut materials, usually metals. By focusing the light into a very small and hot focal point, we can cut metals such as steel, stainless, aluminium, copper and brass.The precision laser profiling that we provide allows us to deliver high-specification and highly optimised metal parts and components.

**Our precision laser profiling includes:**

* Etchings and markings of components.
* Cut complicated shapes without the need for expensive tooling.
* +/- 0.1mm tolerances in many thicknesses.
* Reduced localised heat distortion.
* Polished edge finish on stainless steel.
