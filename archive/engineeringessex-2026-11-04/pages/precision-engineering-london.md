## Precision Engineering in London with CAD Software & CNC

### What is CAD Software?

Computer-aided design (CAD) is the first part of the process of creating your part or component. Using computer software, we design and prepare 2D drawings and 3D models. Using CAD and computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) our precision engineering in London can assist a range of industries. Using One CNC CAD-CAM, a market leader programming system, we deliver high standards, tight tolerances and precision engineering in London.

### What is CNC Machining?

Computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) uses software and computer-controlled machinery to automate a manufacturing process. Unlike CAD, which focuses on the design of a product or part and how it functions, CAM focuses on how to make it. CAM is the controlled way of changing raw materials into shapes using cutting, drilling, boring and more.
