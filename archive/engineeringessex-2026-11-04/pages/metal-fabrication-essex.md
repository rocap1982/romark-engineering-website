### CNC Machining

Computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) uses software and computer-controlled machinery to automate a manufacturing process. Unlike CAD, which focuses on the design of a product or part and how it functions, CAM focuses on how to make it. CAM is the controlled way of changing raw materials into shapes using cutting, drilling, boring and more.
